# 🍲 Food War – Multiplayer Ingredient Guessing Game

**Food War** is a fun, real-time **multiplayer ingredient guessing game** where players compete to identify the ingredients of different dishes.

Players join a shared game room, answer within timed rounds, earn points based on **correctness and speed**, and compete for the highest score on the final leaderboard.

---

## ✨ What You Can Do with Food War

* 🎮 **Real-Time Multiplayer** – Play live with friends or other players in the same room.
* 🏠 **Game Rooms** – Create or join a game using a unique room ID.
* ⏱️ **Timed Rounds** – Guess ingredients before the countdown ends.
* 🧠 **Smart Matching** – Accept close or partial ingredient matches to handle minor spelling mistakes.
* 🏆 **Dynamic Scoring** – Earn more points for faster and correct answers.
* 🔄 **Progressive Rounds** – Move through multiple rounds with synchronized game state.
* 📊 **Live Leaderboard** – Track player scores and see the final rankings.
* ✨ **Interactive UI** – Animated feedback, score updates, alerts, and game effects.
